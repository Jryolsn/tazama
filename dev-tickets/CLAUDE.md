# dev-tickets

This folder is the ticket workspace. One subfolder per ticket, each with a
`PLAN.md` that must be readable cold, without the chat transcript.

Before working on a ticket:

1. Read `PROJECT.md` (project-specific facts, plan language, conventions).
2. Read `README.md` if you have not worked in a ticket workspace before.
3. Read the ticket's `PLAN.md` top to bottom and follow the `## ▶` resume
   section before writing code.

While working:

- Log decisions in `PLAN.md` as they are made, with absolute dates
  (2026-09-05) and the reasoning, never just the outcome.
- Tick off plan steps as they land.
- Findings outside the plan go under `Findings` in the plan. Ask before
  turning one into its own ticket.
- Ask before creating, deleting, or archiving ticket folders.
- New ideas or bugs found along the way go in `../BACKLOG.md`.

Before ending a session, update the `## ▶` section (state, what was done,
exact next step) and the ticket's line in `INDEX.md`.
